IV League is an AI-powered 0DTE options trading system that ranks eight liquid stocks and ETFs using option-chain IV/skew, sells puts on the strongest premium opportunities, and takes selective directional trades on the remaining candidates. Specialized Analyst, Risk Manager, Execution, and Strategist agents work together to analyze, trade, monitor, and continuously improve the strategy.

Architecture

Every morning the Strategy Engine ranks all 8 underlyings by option-chain IV skew: the top 3 go to premium-selling (sell ~15Δ 0DTE puts, cash-secured), the bottom 5 go to the Analyst Agent, which reads news, events, and the first 10 minutes of price action to call each one Bullish/Bearish/Undecided. The Risk Manager sizes and approves both sides against live account balance, the Execution Agent places and monitors every position (~1 minute polling, TP/SL/EOD rules), and the Strategist Agent reviews each day's results after the close, proposing process fixes a human reviews before anything changes live.
